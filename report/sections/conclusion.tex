\section{Conclusion}
The pairwise and order encodings both certify an optimum when a validated
layout meets a lower bound or when the next smaller threshold is
unsatisfiable. On BCSSTK01, order built a smaller formula at the same
threshold, while pairwise reached the stronger incumbent in the 300-second
rerun. The original CP-SAT certificate fixes the optimum at 12. In the
six-method rerun, pairwise SAT, CP-SAT and CP Optimizer each reached 12
without independently proving it. On BP~400, both SAT runs stopped at
the size guard. Evaluating construction cost and proof time on more
instances that reach SAT search remains necessary before choosing one
encoding for larger graphs.
