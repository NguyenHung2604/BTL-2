\section{Conclusion}
At matched thresholds, order produced fewer clauses. Under the same
300-second budget on BCSSTK01, pairwise reached bandwidth 12 and order
reached 13. Their last thresholds were 11 and 12, respectively.
The five-method pilot independently certified 12 with OR-Tools CP-SAT.
Both SAT encodings reached 9 on IBM32 and proved 4 on BCSPWR01 under the
separate 10-second protocol. On BCSPWR02, the 300-second runs improved the identity bandwidth of 24 to 7 (pairwise) and 7 (order). These observations cover four of the six matrix graphs
that actually reached SAT search. They do not establish a general runtime
ranking.
